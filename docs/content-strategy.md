# POYARA — content strategy and publication review (phase 1)

Status: **English copy drafted; publication approval pending**. Updated 2026-10-21.

## One studio, four reading paths

| Reader | First question | Best route | Evidence / next action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Client or commercial partner | What can this studio help me build? | Home → Solutions → Work | Interactive demo → verified contact when available |
| Specialist or potential collaborator | What kind of practice is this? | About → Work → Contact | Founder role, concrete contributions, verified professional links |
| Technical reader | How does the studio think and build? | Insights → Work | Grounded editorial note → live demo |
| International professional evaluator | Is the founder's work credible and attributable? | About → Work → public GitHub | Experience, specific role, inspectable artefact; identity documents stay off-site |

The corporate site is **not** a personal CV, sales funnel packed with buzzwords, or a listing of all unreleased experiments. A clear founder section gives credibility without turning the studio into an individual's job-search page.

## Core message

**POYARA is an independent, founder-led AI-native product studio that makes complex ideas tangible through interactive technology, visual problem-solving and working prototypes.**

Supporting reasons to believe:

1. The founder reports over 14 years of hands-on 3D production experience, including medical and scientific visualization.
2. The public industrial-pump browser demo can be inspected directly; it is a technical prototype, not a claimed commercial deployment.
3. The studio describes limitations and development stage openly.

These are copy inputs, not independently verified employment credentials. Do not invent degrees, employers, certifications, staff, clients, revenue or migration outcomes.

## Evidence and rights

- **Included:** One existing public project at `MaxLightner/industrial-pump-demo`, with a working demo URL and a limited technical description.
- **Not included:** Unreleased applications, pilot customer names, user metrics, private research, client NDA work and unapproved images.
- **Images:** No external project images are embedded during phase 1. Visual assets and usage rights belong to phase 2.
- **Founder:** Avoid a name, portrait, degree, legal entity or location until the preferred public professional identity is explicitly confirmed.
- **Contact:** No invented email address, inactive enquiry form or unverified LinkedIn URL.
