# POYARA — brand decisions (v0.2)

Status: working definition, last reviewed 2026-10-21.

## Identity

- Name: **POYARA** (no “Tech” suffix).
- Positioning: **Independent AI-Native Product Studio**.
- Operating model: founder-led, combining deep 3D/interactive experience,
  AI-enabled workflows, and practical product experimentation.
- Tone: technical, intelligent, independent, experimental, ambitious, and
  evidence-calibrated. No unsupported claims of market traction or team size.
- Language: English-only for the corporate site at launch.

## Who the site serves

1. Clients and commercial collaborators.
2. Specialists and potential project partners.
3. AI/interactive technology readers.
4. International employers, partners and professional evaluators reviewing
   the founder's verifiable work.

The studio website is **not** a personal résumé, but it must give the founder
an accessible, credible professional profile and project-role evidence.
The preferred public professional name is not yet confirmed; do not infer it
from an account handle or use an unverified legal identity.

## Public narrative

Show how real digital products are researched, built and tested, and what
problem each solves. Prioritize substantiated demos and case studies over a
large list of unlaunched products. Mention a founder's exact contribution and
project maturity without inflating commercial success.

## Boundaries

- No public disclosure of unlaunched projects without explicit approval.
- No fake testimonials, clients, team members, metrics, funding or logos.
- No generic “AI will revolutionize everything” copy.
- Education is planned, not publicly advertised as available until content
  exists.
- AI content updates will require review and approval before publication.

## Later decisions

Final logo, palette, founder legal/public display name and biography, verified
contact address, domain, and approved project inventory.

See `content-strategy.md` for audience paths, actual public evidence and the
sign-off list that separates working copy from launch-ready content.
